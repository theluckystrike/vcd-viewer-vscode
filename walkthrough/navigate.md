# Zoom, cursor and markers

| Do this | To |
|---|---|
| Ctrl or Cmd + wheel, or pinch | Zoom at the mouse |
| Drag, Shift + wheel, or the bar under the waves | Move through time |
| Shift + drag, or drag on the time ruler | Zoom to that range |
| `F` or Fit | Show the whole time range |
| Click | Put the cursor there. The Value column shows each signal at the cursor. |
| `A` and `B` | Put marker A or B at the cursor. The toolbar shows B−A. |
| Left and Right arrow | Jump to the previous or next change of the selected signal |
| Home and End | Cursor to the start or the end |
